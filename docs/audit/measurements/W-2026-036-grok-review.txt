1. DOES RULE-019 DEFINE "INDEPENDENT REVIEWER" TIGHTLY, WITH NO LOOPHOLE?
================================================================

No. It is tighter than "session N vs session N+1." It is not tight. Doors:

D1. "مزوّد" is undefined.
    Lab (xAI vs Anthropic)? API vendor (OpenAI vs Azure OpenAI)? Host (Perplexity Computer vs a laptop)? The illustrative list (GPT · Grok · Gemini · Kimi) picks the lab reading — the reading that makes a Grok-on-Perplexity review of an Anthropic-on-Perplexity patch count. The platform reading is the one C-18 actually cares about, and it fails. A definition that is satisfied only under the self-serving reading of an undefined term is a loophole.

D2. "عائلة" is undefined.
    Claude 3 vs Claude 4? GPT-4 vs o-series (same lab)? Llama served by Groq vs Meta? A distilled student of Claude branded as something else? "لا نسخةً من العائلةِ نفسِها" has no test.

D3. Condition 3 is self-assertion.
    "يُكتبُ اسمُ النموذجِ وإصدارُه في تقريرِ المراجعة." Writing "Grok 4.6" is the check. `check_work.mjs` only tests `String(owner).trim() !== String(reviewer).trim()`. Forgery cost: one string.

D4. Condition 4 contains an OR.
    "خرجُهُ الخامُّ أو ملخصٌ قابلٌ للتدقيق." The executor may substitute a summary for the raw output. The party who would be exposed by the raw text is the party who chooses which text is saved.

D5. Condition 2 isolation is a documentary claim about what the reviewer "sees."
    No hash-binding of the prompt, no prohibition on executor-spawned subagents, no prohibition on extra context, no record of tools given. This review is the counterexample.

D6. "يتقدّمُ واحدٌ منها على الآخر" (12-1).
    RULE-019 says the five are required ("يُشترط"). §12-1 adds ranking language. Ranked conditions are how later conditions get dropped when inconvenient. Ambiguity in a rule that exists to close an identity gap is a loophole.

D7. Optionality.
    RULE-019 defines when a review *counts as* independent. It does not say every RULE-004 review must meet it. WORK_CONTROL §4 step 5 is unchanged: owner ≠ reviewer as strings. Default path remains session-number theatre. The new title is an optional costume.

D8. No disagreement rule, no retry cap, no anti-selection rule.
    Work order W-2026-036 says both GPT and Grok must accept. RULE-019 and §12 do not. Executor may spawn, discard, rerun, and keep the ACCEPTED copy. Nothing forbids that.

Tight vs "different session"? Yes.
Tight vs circumvention? No.

2. IS THE FUNCTIONAL/MODEL vs ORGANIZATIONAL DISTINCTION HONEST, OR DOES IT CLAIM WHAT IT DOES NOT HAVE?
================================================================

The distinction as a *description of facts* is honest: a different lab's model, hosted by the same operator, is not an external organization. §12-2 says the platform and the operator remain one. That sentence is true.

The distinction as *used* is misleading, because of three surrounding moves.

M1. The weasel-if.
    RULE-019 / §12-2: "فإن طُلب «جهةٌ خارجيّةٌ تنظيميًّا» بالمعنى الأشدّ … وإلّا تُعلنُ الفجوةُ ولا تُغلقُ بالادّعاء."
    Organizational independence is framed as a hypothetical future request.
    It is not hypothetical. It is the existing criterion.
    C-18 title: "تدقيق مستقل خارج سلطة المنفّذين بلا فجوة."
    C-18.md: "يلزم شهادةُ استقلالٍ من جهةٍ غير كاتب هذه الملفات: مراجعةٌ خارجية أو أمرٌ ملكي يعتمد نتيجة التدقيق بعد قراءتها."
    T-115ب (COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md external-conditions table): "جهةٌ مستقلّةٌ عن مالكي المستودع (طرفٌ ثانٍ مُشغِّل)" — a real second operator, not a test simulation.
    docs/audit/independence/README.md §3: the machine is not the certificate; a single operator can carry both digital identities; C-18 remains a gap; promoting C-18 because the machine exists would violate RULE-004.
    So: C-18 already "requested" the stronger meaning. The "if" demotes a standing requirement to an optional upgrade.

M2. The object substitution.
    T-115 as recorded in verdict.md is two clauses: (a) no independence certificate for the audit from a party other than its author; (b) role separation declared in files, not implemented in actor identity.
    T-115 was already split: T-115أ (machine, closed) and T-115ب (the certificate, open, real second party).
    W-2026-036 reinterprets "هوية الفاعل" as *model-lab identity of a work-item reviewer*, then binds that reinterpretation to T-115/C-18.
    Those are different objects. A Grok review of a governance paragraph is not an independent audit of 21 criteria, is not IndependenceRegistry.attest, is not a royal order, and is not a second operator.

M3. Claiming the cause is closed while claiming the ticket is open.
    Commit d18d4bf body, first sentence: "أُغلقت علّةُ «فصلِ الأدوارِ معلنٌ في الملفّاتِ لا منفَّذٌ في هويةِ الفاعل» (فجوةُ C-18/T-115)."
    Later: "ولا يُغلقُ T-115 بهذا البندِ وحده."
    Closing the *cause* in git history while leaving the *ticket* nominally open is how you get to close the ticket later with a weaker certificate. That is claiming what it does not have.

Does it claim what it doesn't have? The body of RULE-019 tries not to. The commit message does. §12-3 does (see Q5). The evidence package's title "للمراجعة المستقلة متعددة النماذج (RULE-019)" and the work-item reviewer field already treat this process as the independent reviewer for T-115. The distinction is honest in one paragraph and spent in the next.

3. IS §12-4 (NO AUTOMATED ENFORCEMENT OF MODEL PROVIDER) A GENUINE LIMIT OR A VULNERABILITY?
================================================================

It is a genuine description of a vulnerability. Declaring a hole does not fill it. Analogizing it to PA-09 is false.

PA-09 (W-2026-014): a real control in `scripts/push_api.mjs` that compares trees after a real push. It is untested in unit tests because a unit test does not call the network. The guard exists in production; the test cannot reach it. That is a measurement limit of a live control.

RULE-019: no control in `check_work.mjs` at all. The only automated identity check remains string inequality of owner vs reviewer — the exact check T-115 already diagnosed as insufficient. §12-4 is not "a guard we cannot unit-test." It is "we did not build the guard."

GOVERNING_RULE.md, immediately above the work-control rules, including the new RULE-019:

    "هذه القواعد تُنفَّذ آليًّا بـ`scripts/check_work.mjs` … وما لا حاجزَ له ليس قاعدةً بل أمنيّةٌ بصيغة الأمر."

RULE-019 was inserted into the section that claims automatic enforcement, and §12-4 admits it has none. By the file's own meta-rule, RULE-019 is not a rule. It is an exhortation in the form of a command. T-115 was exactly this: separation declared in files, not enforced in actor identity. The "fix" is a more elaborate declaration plus an explicit non-enforcement clause.

T-115 reproduced at higher linguistic resolution.

§12-4 is therefore:
- honest as disclosure,
- false as PA-09 analogy,
- fatal as a response to T-115,
- a vulnerability, not a "حدٌّ صادق" in the sense that would make the change adequate.

A declared unenforced identity rule cannot close an "unenforced identity" gap.

5. DOES THE DEFINITION CLOSE T-115/C-18 BY EXISTING, OR OPEN A DOOR TO CLOSING IT BY ASSERTION?
================================================================

It does not close by mere existence. That sentence in RULE-019 is true and cheap.

It opens a door to closing by assertion. That door is §12-3, and it is the defect that matters.

§12-3, verbatim structure:
    NOT closed by defining the protocol.
    IS closed by issuing an actual review certificate meeting 12-1 on a specific work item, recording model, raw output, and accept/reject.

12-1 is model-provider diversity plus paperwork.
C-18 is an independence certificate for the *audit*, from a party outside executor authority, or a royal order adopting the audit after reading it.
T-115ب is a real second operator calling IndependenceRegistry.attest on the text of verdict.md.

§12-3 names T-115/C-18 as the thing that closes, and 12-1 as the sufficient condition. That is a redefinition of the gap, followed by a closure path under the new definition. Redefinition-then-certificate is closing by assertion with extra steps.

The evidence package, work order, and commit already treat "GPT 5.6 + Grok 4.6 accept this patch" as the next step toward that certificate. If both files say ACCEPTED, nothing in RULE-019 or §12 prevents someone from writing "T-115 closed: see W-2026-036-grok-review.txt." C-18.md would still be unsatisfied. The table in COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md would still list T-115ب as an external condition requiring a second operator. The protocol does not mention T-115ب at all.

Further: using RULE-019 to bless RULE-019 is self-attestation one level up. C-18.md already forbade the pattern: "هذه المرحلة هي التدقيق، والتدقيق لا يشهد لاستقلاله بأداةٍ من عنده." A definition of independence cannot be the first object certified under that definition for the purpose of later closing the independence gap.

So: not closed by existing. Opened to closure by assertion, on the wrong object, under a weaker standard, by a process that cannot prove its own independence.
