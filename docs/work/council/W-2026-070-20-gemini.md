## 2. Findings and Evidence

### Defect 1: `INCOMPLETE_DIAGNOSIS`
- **Claim in Work Item:**
  W-2026-070 claims to have identified the root cause of variance in full-suite runs (`npm test` / `npm run verify`):
  `scripts/check_declaration.sh:172` (`elif printf '%s\n' "$closed_ids" | grep -qx "$t"; then`) causing `SIGPIPE` (exit code 141) under `pipefail`, falling through to `else` and emitting `[DC-04]`.
- **Finding:**
  The diagnosis is incomplete. It failed to identify line 153 of `scripts/check_declaration.sh`:
  ```bash
  152:      for v in $(grep -o 'T-[0-9]\+' "$vp" | sort -u || true); do
  153:        if ! printf '%s\n' "$cell_ids" | grep -qx "$v"; then
  154:          echo "✗ [DC-03] $cid: حكمُ التدقيق في $vp يذكر $v وخانةُ «البند المفتوح» لا تذكره"
  ```
  Line 153 uses the exact same pattern: `printf '%s\n' "$cell_ids" | grep -qx "$v"`. When `cell_ids` contains multiple entries (e.g. `T-27\nT-29` for criterion `C-08`), `grep` matches `T-27` and exits immediately. `printf` is killed with `SIGPIPE` (141). Under `set -o pipefail`, the pipeline evaluates to 141. The bash logical negation `!` turns non-zero 141 into 0 (true), triggering the error block and emitting `[DC-03]` false positive failures.
- **Evidence:**
  1. In our direct execution of `npm run verify` (Run 1), the suite failed specifically due to line 153 emitting:
     `✗ [DC-03] C-08: حكمُ التدقيق في docs/audit/criteria/C-08.md يذكر T-27 وخانةُ «البند المفتوح» لا تذكره`.
  2. In our 200-run parallel stress probe (8 workers × 25 rounds), out of 30 failures, exactly 15 were `[DC-03]` (from line 153) and 15 were `[DC-04]` (from line 172):
     - `[DC-03] C-08` (9 occurrences), `[DC-03] C-12` (5 occurrences), `[DC-03] C-21` (2 occurrences).
     - `[DC-04] C-09/C-12/C-13/C-20` (15 occurrences).
  3. The proposed remediation in W-2026-070 only addresses line 172. If implemented without fixing line 153, exactly half of the intermittent variance remains unaddressed.
