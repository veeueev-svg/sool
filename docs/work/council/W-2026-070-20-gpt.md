## Findings

### SIGPIPE_ROOT_CAUSE_PARTIALLY_CORRECT

The central mechanism is real.  `scripts/check_declaration.sh` enables `set -euo pipefail` at line 53.  A successful early `grep -q` may close the pipe before `printf` finishes; `printf=141` makes the pipe status nonzero and changes a conditional result.  The direct retained-probe rerun and the 19 stress failures establish this with raw measurements.

### INCOMPLETE_PIPEFAIL_SITE_INVENTORY

The diagnosis is not complete and its “line 172 root cause” claim is materially too narrow.  At this frozen commit the same vulnerable `printf | grep -qx` construction exists at all three of these executable sites under the same `pipefail`:

* line 153: `if ! printf '%s\n' "$cell_ids" | grep -qx "$v"; then`
* line 172: `elif printf '%s\n' "$closed_ids" | grep -qx "$t"; then`
* line 369: `if printf '%s\n' "$complete_phases" | grep -qx "$p"; then`

The stress measurement directly proves line 153 is not merely a theoretical sibling: its altered condition emits the `[DC-03]` false failures listed above.  Line 172 produces the observed `[DC-04]` failures.  Line 369 remains an unremediated same-mechanism site; when a partial phase is also present in the completed list, an early matching `grep` can suppress the intended conflict detection.  The report does say to “review every `printf | grep -q`,” but that is a future recommendation, not the required complete diagnosis of every variance-producing site.

### ACCEPTANCE_VERIFY_ZERO_FALSE

The acceptance criterion says `npm run verify ⇒ EXIT=0` at the work head.  On the actual frozen commit, I directly captured `npm run verify` exit `1`; `npm test` also directly exited `1`.  This fails the criterion.  The evidence’s claimed green verify run is additionally tied to `10a1560`, while the retained diagnosis is tied to `7e1f96f`, neither of which is the frozen `7f81def...` commit under review.

The acceptance standard requires a diagnosis measured on the same commit.  The evidence package freezes review at `7f81def...`, but its report and raw measurement identify `7e1f96f` and describe initial work on `16b5a10`.  No retained raw measurement establishes the item’s numerical claims on `7f81def...`.  My measurements show the behavior continues, but cannot cure this evidentiary defect in the submitted diagnosis.

## Acceptance judgment

The item satisfies neither the complete-diagnosis requirement nor the `npm run verify` exit-zero requirement at the frozen commit.
